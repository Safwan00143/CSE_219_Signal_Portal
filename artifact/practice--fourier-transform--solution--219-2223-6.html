**(d)** By **linearity** and the results of (b), $$\begin{aligned}&\mathcal F\{f(t)-f(-t)\}\\[6pt]&= \frac1{a+j\omega}-\frac1{a-j\omega}\\[6pt]&= \frac{(a-j\omega)-(a+j\omega)}{a^2+\omega^2}\\[6pt]&= \frac{-2j\omega}{a^2+\omega^2}.\end{aligned}$$ Take the limit $a\to0^+$: $$\begin{aligned}&\mathcal F\{\operatorname{sign}(t)\}\\[6pt]&= \lim_{a\to0}\frac{-2j\omega}{a^2+\omega^2}\\[6pt]&= \frac{-2j}{\omega}\\[6pt]&= \boxed{\frac{2}{j\omega}}\end{aligned}$$ **Properties used:** time reversal, linearity, and taking a limit of transforms (a sign function is not absolutely integrable, so its transform is defined as this limit). *Bonus:* since $u(t)=\tfrac12+\tfrac12\operatorname{sign}(t)$, this gives $U(j\omega)=\pi\delta(\omega)+\frac1{j\omega}$.
