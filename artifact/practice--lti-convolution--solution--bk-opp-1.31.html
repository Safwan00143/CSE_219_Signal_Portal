We never need h(t)h(t)h(t). Linearity and time invariance say that if an input is a combination of shifted copies of x1x_1x1​, the output is the same combination of shifted copies of y1y_1y1​:

∑iai x1(t−ti) ⟶ ∑iai y1(t−ti).\sum_i a_i\,x_1(t-t_i)\ \longrightarrow\ \sum_i a_i\,y_1(t-t_i).∑i​ai​x1​(t−ti​) ⟶ ∑i​ai​y1​(t−ti​).

So the task is to write x2x_2x2​ and x3x_3x3​ in terms of x1x_1x1​, a unit pulse on 0<t<20<t<20<t<2.

(a) x2x_2x2​ is +1+1+1 on (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) and −1-1−1 on (2,4)(2,4)(2,4):

x2(t)=x1(t)−x1(t−2) ⇒ y2(t)=y1(t)−y1(t−2).x_2(t)=x_1(t)-x_1(t-2)\ \Rightarrow\ y_2(t)=y_1(t)-y_1(t-2).x2​(t)=x1​(t)−x1​(t−2) ⇒ y2​(t)=y1​(t)−y1​(t−2).

That is the triangle on (0,2)(0,2)(0,2) with peak 2, followed by an upside-down triangle on (2,4)(2,4)(2,4) with peak −2-2−2.

(b) x3x_3x3​ is 1 on (−1,0)(-1,0)(−1,0), 2 on (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) and 1 on (1,2)(1,2)(1,2). That is two overlapping pulses: one on (−1,1)(-1,1)(−1,1) and one on (0,2)(0,2)(0,2):

x3(t)=x1(t+1)+x1(t) ⇒ y3(t)=y1(t+1)+y1(t).x_3(t)=x_1(t+1)+x_1(t)\ \Rightarrow\ y_3(t)=y_1(t+1)+y_1(t).x3​(t)=x1​(t+1)+x1​(t) ⇒ y3​(t)=y1​(t+1)+y1​(t).

Adding the triangles piece by piece:

  • −1<t<0-1<t<0−1<t<0: 2(t+1)2(t+1)2(t+1);
  • 0<t<10<t<10<t<1: 2(1−t)+2t=22(1-t)+2t=22(1−t)+2t=2;
  • 1<t<21<t<21<t<2: 2(2−t)2(2-t)2(2−t).

The result is a trapezoid.
