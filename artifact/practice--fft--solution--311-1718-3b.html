Formulation (DIF). Split the time sequence into first and second halves, then separate even and odd outputs:

Gk=∑n=0N/2−1[gn+(−1)k gn+N/2]WNkn,(WNkN/2=(−1)k).G_k=\sum_{n=0}^{N/2-1}\Big[g_n+(-1)^k\,g_{n+N/2}\Big]W_N^{kn},\qquad (W_N^{kN/2}=(-1)^k).Gk​=n=0∑N/2−1​[gn​+(−1)kgn+N/2​]WNkn​,(WNkN/2​=(−1)k).
  • Even outputs k=2rk=2rk=2r: WN2rn=WN/2rnW_N^{2rn}=W_{N/2}^{rn}WN2rn​=WN/2rn​, so G2r=∑n=0N/2−1an WN/2rn,an=gn+gn+N/2.G_{2r}=\sum_{n=0}^{N/2-1}a_n\,W_{N/2}^{rn},\qquad a_n=g_n+g_{n+N/2}.G2r​=∑n=0N/2−1​an​WN/2rn​,an​=gn​+gn+N/2​.
  • Odd outputs k=2r+1k=2r+1k=2r+1: G2r+1=∑n=0N/2−1bn WN/2rn,bn=(gn−gn+N/2)WN n.G_{2r+1}=\sum_{n=0}^{N/2-1}b_n\,W_{N/2}^{rn},\qquad b_n=\big(g_n-g_{n+N/2}\big)W_N^{\,n}.G2r+1​=∑n=0N/2−1​bn​WN/2rn​,bn​=(gn​−gn+N/2​)WNn​.

So one NNN-point DFT becomes two N2\frac N22N​-point DFTs, after N2\frac N22N​ DIF butterflies:

an=gn+gn+N/2,bn=(gn−gn+N/2) WNn.a_n=g_n+g_{n+N/2},\qquad b_n=(g_n-g_{n+N/2})\,W_N^{n}.an​=gn​+gn+N/2​,bn​=(gn​−gn+N/2​)WNn​.

Here the twiddle multiplies after the subtraction; in DIT it comes before. Repeat on each half until 2-point DFTs remain: log⁡2N\log_2Nlog2​N stages.

With in-place computation, each butterfly overwrites the two memory cells it read, so no extra array is needed and results stay at the positions where the flow graph produces them. Write the memory position in binary and reverse its bits to get the index of the GkG_kGk​ stored there:

Why: the first stage separates even from odd kkk (the last bit of kkk decides the top or bottom half), the next stage separates by the second-to-last bit, and so on. The least significant bit of kkk ends up deciding the most significant bit of the position. A final bit-reversal permutation restores natural order. DIT is the mirror image: bit-reversed input, natural-order output.
