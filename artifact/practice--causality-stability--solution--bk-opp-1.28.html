(a) Reversal: linear and stable, but not TI and not causal (y[−1]=x[1]y[-1]=x[1]y[−1]=x[1]). It has memory.

(b) A fixed combination of delayed inputs. It is the impulse response h[n]=δ[n−2]−2δ[n−8]h[n]=\delta[n-2]-2\delta[n-8]h[n]=δ[n−2]−2δ[n−8], so it is TI, linear, causal and stable (∑∣h∣=3\sum|h|=3∑∣h∣=3). It has memory.

(c) A time-varying gain nnn. It is memoryless, linear and causal. It is not TI, and not stable: x=1x=1x=1 gives y=ny=ny=n.
