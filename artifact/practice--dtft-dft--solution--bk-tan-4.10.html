Bin kkk of an NNN-point DFT corresponds to the frequency fk=kfsNf_k=\frac{kf_s}{N}fk​=Nkfs​​. So:

  • the frequency resolution, the spacing between bins, is Δf=fsN\Delta f=\frac{f_s}{N}Δf=Nfs​​;
  • the folding frequency (the Nyquist limit), above which the bins mirror the lower half, is fs2\frac{f_s}22fs​​.
