The highest frequency represented is $\frac1{2T_s}=0.5$ Hz. **(iv) Choosing $T_s$ and $T_0$.** - **$T_s\le\frac1{2B}$** (sampling above the Nyquist rate) to avoid aliasing. For non-band-limited signals choose $T_s$ small enough that $G(f)$ is negligible beyond $\frac1{2T_s}$, and use an anti-aliasing filter. - **$T_0$ long enough** to (a) cover the whole signal, so truncation does not cause leakage, and (b) give the required **frequency resolution** $f_0=1/T_0$. For long signals, windowing reduces leakage from truncation. - **$N_0=T_0/T_s$** should be a power of 2 for the radix-2 FFT. If needed, **zero-pad**: this increases $T_0$ and gives finer spectral samples without changing the underlying spectrum.
