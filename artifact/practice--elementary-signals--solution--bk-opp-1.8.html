Two identities do all the work:

  • a minus sign is a phase of π\piπ: −cos⁡θ=cos⁡(θ+π)-\cos\theta=\cos(\theta+\pi)−cosθ=cos(θ+π);
  • a sine is a shifted cosine: sin⁡θ=cos⁡(θ−π2)\sin\theta=\cos\left(\theta-\frac\pi2\right)sinθ=cos(θ−2π​), so −sin⁡θ=cos⁡(θ+π2)-\sin\theta=\cos\left(\theta+\frac\pi2\right)−sinθ=cos(θ+2π​).
