Why frequency domain?

  • LTI systems act on each frequency independently: Y(f)=H(f)G(f)Y(f)=H(f)G(f)Y(f)=H(f)G(f). Convolution becomes multiplication.
  • Bandwidth, filtering, modulation and channel sharing (FDM) are all naturally described in frequency.
  • It shows how much of the band a signal occupies, and which components a channel will pass or distort.

So periodic signals have energy only at harmonics, while aperiodic signals spread energy over a continuum.

Bandwidth. No. The envelope of both spectra is the same sinc, set by the pulse width τ\tauτ: first null at 1/τ1/\tau1/τ, so the essential bandwidth is about 1/τ1/\tau1/τ in both cases. The period only sets the spacing of the lines. Making τ\tauτ smaller widens the bandwidth whether or not the pulse repeats.
