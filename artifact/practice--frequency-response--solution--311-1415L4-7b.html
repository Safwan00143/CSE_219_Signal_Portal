Why "ideal". The output appearing before the input means the filter is non-causal: its impulse response, a sinc, exists for t<0t<0t<0. No physical device can respond to an input it has not yet received. The brick-wall response, perfect flat passband, zero transition band and infinite stop-band attenuation, is a mathematical idealisation used for analysis. Real filters only approximate it with finite roll-off and some phase non-linearity.
