Part 1 — frequency to time (the convolution theorem). Let y(t)=F−1{G(f)H(f)}y(t)=\mathcal F^{-1}\{G(f)H(f)\}y(t)=F−1{G(f)H(f)}. Write H(f)=∫h(τ)e−j2πfτdτH(f)=\int h(\tau)e^{-j2\pi f\tau}d\tauH(f)=∫h(τ)e−j2πfτdτ:

y(t)=∫−∞∞G(f)[∫−∞∞h(τ)e−j2πfτdτ]ej2πftdf=∫−∞∞h(τ)[∫−∞∞G(f)ej2πf(t−τ)df]⏟g(t−τ)dτ.\begin{aligned}&y(t)\\[6pt]&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}G(f)\left[\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}h(\tau)e^{-j2\pi f\tau}d\tau\right]e^{j2\pi ft}df\\[6pt]&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}h(\tau)\underbrace{\left[\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}G(f)e^{j2\pi f(t-\tau)}df\right]}_{g(t-\tau)}d\tau .\end{aligned}​y(t)=∫−∞∞​G(f)[∫−∞∞​h(τ)e−j2πfτdτ]ej2πftdf=∫−∞∞​h(τ)g(t−τ)[∫−∞∞​G(f)ej2πf(t−τ)df]​​dτ.​

Hence

y(t)=∫−∞∞h(τ) g(t−τ) dτ=g(t)∗h(t)\boxed{y(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}h(\tau)\,g(t-\tau)\,d\tau=g(t)*h(t)}y(t)=∫−∞∞​h(τ)g(t−τ)dτ=g(t)∗h(t)​

Part 2 — time domain only. Let the system be linear and time invariant, with δ(t)→h(t)\delta(t)\to h(t)δ(t)→h(t).

  1. Sifting. Any input is a continuum of weighted, shifted impulses: g(t)=∫−∞∞g(τ) δ(t−τ) dτ.g(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(\tau)\,\delta(t-\tau)\,d\tau .g(t)=∫−∞∞​g(τ)δ(t−τ)dτ.
  2. Time invariance. δ(t−τ) → h(t−τ)\delta(t-\tau)\ \to\ h(t-\tau)δ(t−τ) → h(t−τ).
  3. Homogeneity. The constant weight g(τ) dτg(\tau)\,d\taug(τ)dτ passes through: g(τ)δ(t−τ)dτ → g(τ)h(t−τ)dτg(\tau)\delta(t-\tau)d\tau\ \to\ g(\tau)h(t-\tau)d\taug(τ)δ(t−τ)dτ → g(τ)h(t−τ)dτ.
  4. Additivity. The integral is the limit of a sum of such terms, so the outputs add: y(t)=∫−∞∞g(τ) h(t−τ) dτ=g(t)∗h(t).\begin{aligned}&y(t)\\[6pt]&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(\tau)\,h(t-\tau)\,d\tau\\[6pt]&= g(t)*h(t).\end{aligned}​y(t)=∫−∞∞​g(τ)h(t−τ)dτ=g(t)∗h(t).​

This is the same as Part 1. Filtering in frequency (multiply by HHH) and passing through the LTI system in time (convolve with hhh) are the same operation.
