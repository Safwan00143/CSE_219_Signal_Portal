Complex multiplications. Each stage has N2\frac N22N​ butterflies with one twiddle each, and there are log⁡2N\log_2Nlog2​N stages:

N2log⁡2N=2×2=4.\begin{aligned}&\frac N2\log_2N\\[6pt]&= 2\times2\\[6pt]&= 4.\end{aligned}​2N​log2​N=2×2=4.​
